04 · Experience

Research & teaching

  1. Sep 2024 – present · Supervisor: Prof. A. K. M. Abdul Malek Azad

    Brain–computer interfaced wheelchair for quadriplegia patients

    • Real-time EMG blink-intent detection with cascaded Butterworth band-pass and 50 Hz notch filtering: 93% accuracy at 0.45 s latency across 15 participants.
    • Scanning control interface in real-time firmware (Arduino/ESP) for single-blink wheelchair driving.
    • Obstacle-avoidance layer; ToF sensing responded 13% faster than ultrasonic.

  2. Nov 2024 – May 2025 · Supervisor: Dr. A. S. Nazmul Huda

    Demand-side management & optimal sizing of an industrial hybrid microgrid

    • Machine-level load data from a 1.44 MW-peak factory; 40 loads classed as critical, shiftable or clippable.
    • Combined DSM strategy cut the daily bill by >30% and NPC by ~18%, raising renewable fraction from 57.8% to 71.9%.
    • PSO, GA and DE sizing: GA design 7.58% cheaper than HOMER, with a 44% smaller diesel generator.

  3. Oct 2023 – Sep 2024 · Supervisor: Prof. A. K. M. Abdul Malek Azad

    IoT-driven precision agriculture

    • Landsat-8 NDVI/NDSI + K-means vegetation and salinity mapping, with LSTM forecasting.
    • ESP32 soil unit with cloud ML; Random Forest chosen from eight models (97.35%, 22 crops).
    • Solar fuzzy-logic irrigation used ~70% less water than threshold control. Published in PLOS ONE.
